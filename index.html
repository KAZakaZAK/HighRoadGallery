Our mission and vision

High Road Gallery is a community art space where artists create, exhibit and connect. In our 1818 historic home, we support both emerging and established artists, offer a welcoming place for community connection and steward a meaningful piece of Worthington's history.

Vision. We envision our historic 1818 home continuing to serve as a place where artists and community members gather to nurture beauty and belonging.
